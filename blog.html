Bolt now runs in Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot

Build and ship real apps right from the tools your team already uses — no tab-switching, no context loss, no separate tool to adopt.

👤
Eric Simons
6 min read
Hero image

Today we're announcing that Bolt is now available natively inside Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot. You can describe an app, watch it get built in real time, and share a working preview — all without leaving the conversation.

This is the result of a strategic partnership with Microsoft, and it changes something fundamental about how teams build internal tools: the moment of inspiration and the moment of creation are now the same moment.

Why Teams?

Most software ideas die in chat. Someone mentions a problem, a few people react, someone says "we should build something for that," and then... nothing. The friction between the idea and the first line of code is too high. By the time a ticket is filed and a sprint is planned, the energy is gone.

Bolt in Teams removes that friction entirely. You can describe what you want in plain English, in the same message thread where the problem was raised, and have a working prototype to share in minutes.

"The gap between idea and working software just collapsed. That's not a small thing."

How it works

When Bolt is enabled for your organization, a @Bolt mention in any Teams chat opens a build session. Bolt uses Copilot's language understanding to turn your description into a detailed spec, then builds the app step by step — visible to everyone in the thread.

  • Describe your app in plain English — no spec doc, no wireframes required
  • Watch Bolt plan, scaffold, and build in real time
  • Get a live preview link that works on any device
  • Iterate with follow-up messages, just like a conversation
Try Bolt in Microsoft Teams

Available now for enterprise organizations. Contact our team to enable it for your workspace.

Talk to our teamTalk to our team

Built for enterprise from day one

Access is controlled through Microsoft Entra ID. Your IT admin enables Bolt for the organization and governs who can use it — no separate user management, no shadow IT, no new passwords to remember.

Everything runs in your tenant. App previews are scoped to your organization. Your existing security posture applies automatically.

$ bolt deploy --env production --org acme-corp ✓ Building application... ✓ Deploying to acme-corp workspace... ✓ Preview available at: https://bolt.new/preview/x7k2m

What's available today

The Teams integration is live now for enterprise customers. The Microsoft 365 Copilot integration is in private beta — you can request access from the link below. We're letting people in in batches, and we'll reach out when your spot opens.
