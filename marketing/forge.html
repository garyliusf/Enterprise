We pass these savings to you

Running open models on our own capacity costs us less than renting frontier inference. That difference lands in your token balance, not our margin.

01

Dedicated capacity

We run Forge on reserved GPU capacity instead of paying per-call API rates. Fixed cost, predictable throughput, and none of the per-token markup that gets passed down a rental chain.

02

Trained on millions of real builds

Forge models are tuned on the work Bolt actually does: scaffolding, refactors, wiring databases, fixing its own build errors. A smaller model that has seen the task beats a larger one that hasn’t.

03

Rate consistency

One rate, published, no surge pricing and no quiet changes at renewal. When our costs drop, the Forge allowance goes up rather than the price staying put.

Powered by WebContainers

We can offer this at a price no one else can. Your builds run in your browser on our WebContainers runtime, not on rented VMs, and Forge is a working lab where your builds help train and improve open AI models.
