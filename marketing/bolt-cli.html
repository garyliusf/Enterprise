Bolt, from your terminal

Push your local code into Bolt, pull it back down, and manage projects, design systems, and teams — all scriptable, all from the command line.

~ bolt push ./my-project
✓ Synced 24 files → bolt.new/project/a7f3c
~ bolt pull project/a7f3c --out ./local
✓ 24 files written to ./local
~ bolt chat --project=a7f3c
Fetching chat history… 14 messages
~

What it unlocks

Stop bouncing to the browser

Bring Bolt into the workflow you already have.

01

Your code, in and out

Send a local folder up to Bolt, and pull a project back down to your machine. Your editor, your git history, your terminal.

02

Design systems

Spin up design systems from your real sources and kick off generation — without leaving the keyboard.

03

Your chats, on tap

Pull up the full history of any project's chat whenever you need it, ready to search, save, or hand to another tool.

04

Built for teams

Work in your personal account or your org. Set it once and everything follows, so teammates stay on the same page.

05

Secure sign-in

One browser sign-in and you're in. No API keys to copy around, nothing secret living in your repo.

06

Automate anything

It's a CLI, so it scripts. Wire Bolt into your pipelines, cron jobs, and one-off scripts however you like.
