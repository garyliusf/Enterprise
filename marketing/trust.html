Trust and security at Bolt

Millions of people build on Bolt, and a growing number of them are companies. This is how we protect what you build.

Your data

Your data, and how long we keep it

We log authentication events, data access, and privilege escalations, keep them for six months minimum, and can query them during an investigation.

01

Encrypted, always

AES-256 or better at rest. TLS 1.2 or higher in transit.

02

Prompts: 90 days

We keep prompts for 90 days, then delete them permanently from all systems.

03

Offboarding: 30 days

When a contract ends, we delete customer data within 30 days.

04

Least privilege

Need-to-know access, reviewed annually, revoked the moment someone leaves.

Access controls

Controls your IT team expects

01

Single sign-on

SAML 2.0 with any compliant identity provider. OAuth 2.0 supported. Accounts can be locked to SSO-only.

02

Enforced MFA

Require multi-factor authentication through your own identity provider.

03

Role-based access

RBAC across all services, with custom roles for application-specific access.

Where your projects live

Geographic redundancy

Hosted on AWS US-West-2 with backups in US-West-1.

Tenant isolation

Production fully isolated from dev and QA. MFA-only access.

24-hour RTO / RPO

Recovery objectives met, plans tested annually.

Edge defence

AWS Shield, Cloudflare, and AWS WAF. Deny-by-default at every boundary.

AI and your code

Your code is not training data

Bolt does not train models on your code, your prompts, or your outputs. We run every model with training mode off, and we don’t build or host models of our own: code generation runs through Anthropic and Google APIs, used as-is.

01

Never trained on

We never use your inputs, outputs, or metadata to train or fine-tune any model, ours or a vendor’s.

02

Isolated per customer

Model instances are isolated. Prompt sessions never cross customer accounts.

03

Indemnified

Our provider contracts indemnify customers against copyright claims from model training data.

04

Certified providers

Anthropic and Google both hold ISO 42001 for AI management systems.

We maintain a formal Bolt AI Policy, and models are updated only after a new version clears our own evaluation process.

How we work

Continuous monitoring

Monthly vulnerability scans, criticals patched out of cycle. 24/7 security operations team.

Third-party audits

Independent annual assessments. Pentest summaries available on request.

Secure SDLC

A formal Secure Software Development Lifecycle policy with code review in every workflow.

People vetting

Security training, background checks, and NDAs for every employee and contractor.

DPAs with Standard Contractual Clauses are available through your account executive. Breach notification procedures meet GDPR and CCPA requirements.
