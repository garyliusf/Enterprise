How It Works

Up and running in minutes

No new tools to learn. No separate logins. Bolt lives inside the Microsoft products your team already pays for.

01

Admin enables Bolt in Teams

Your IT administrator installs Bolt from the Microsoft Teams App Store and configures access via Entra ID — no additional infrastructure required.

02

Sign in with Microsoft

Team members sign in with their existing Microsoft credentials. No new passwords. No separate account. Identity is managed through your existing Entra tenant.

03

Build inside Teams or Copilot

Describe what you want to build in plain language — directly in a Teams channel or through Microsoft 365 Copilot. Bolt generates a working, production-ready app.

04

Iterate and ship

Refine your app with follow-up prompts, connect it to Azure or GitHub, and deploy — all without switching tools or waiting on engineering.
