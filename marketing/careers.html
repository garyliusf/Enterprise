The team

Who we are

We’re a team of builders, designers, and dreamers reinventing how software is created. We move fast and win as a team, shipping bold ideas that make development faster, more joyful, and more accessible for the next generation of creators.

How we operate

Five values we actually use

Not a poster in a lobby we don’t have. These are the words that come up in code review, in planning, and in the conversation about whether to ship on Friday.

01

Extreme Ownership

Ownership here isn’t a title, it’s a mindset, see what needs to happen, make it happen, carry it to the finish.

02

Win Together

We win together, that means jumping in wherever we’re needed and wearing as many hats as it takes to get there.

03

Bias for Momentum

We keep the pace fast and the feedback loop tight, ship, learn, adjust, repeat.

04

Build for Impact

Impact starts with listening, we build what solves real problems and sweat every detail to get it right.

05

All In

All-in means being present when it counts and human the rest of the time, we take care of each other and respect boundaries.

Perks & benefits

Taken care of, so you can do the work

Health, retirement, time off, and the setup to do good work from wherever you are.

Health coverage

Medical, dental, and vision for you and your dependents, with the company covering the large majority of the premium.

401(k)

A retirement plan you can contribute to from your first paycheck, with no waiting period to enroll.

Wellness

A monthly stipend for whatever keeps you functioning — the gym, therapy, the climbing membership, the good mattress.

Internet reimbursement

If you work from home, your connection is a work tool. We pay for it monthly — no expense-report theatre.

Parental leave

Paid leave for every new parent — birth, adoption, or surrogacy — and a ramp back that assumes you have a newborn, because you do.

Flexible PTO

No fixed allotment, and — more importantly — managers who take their own time off so you feel able to take yours.

How we think about pay.

Equity

Every full-time employee gets it. You should own a piece of what you’re building, not just be paid to build it.

Bands, not haggling

Each role has a level and a range attached before we ever speak to you. Your offer comes from the band — not from how hard you push.

Told up front

The range is on the job post. You’ll know the number before you invest hours in an interview loop.

Hiring process

What to expect

Typically a recruiter conversation, a call with the hiring manager, a practical exercise or portfolio walkthrough that reflects the actual job, and a short set of team conversations. Most loops finish inside two to three weeks. The exact steps are listed on each job post.
