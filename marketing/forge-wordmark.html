Forge, condensed

Three directions survived review: the Inter sans, the Cormorant italic, and Bebas Neue — uppercase-only, so Forge sets as caps against the mark’s lowercase. The sans and serif rounds lead the page, then those two through the six ramps, then twenty more from the refined tier — serious grotesques and serious serifs.
