Gradient

White into brand blue across the word. Forge reads as the new thing without a divider or a badge doing the work. One caveat worth deciding on: the gradient has very little room to travel at navbar size — see the 24px row below.

Cormorant italic

A warmer, editorial Forge against the mark’s hard geometry — the contrast is the idea. Note it’s off-system today: Cormorant is our blog and pull-quote face, so adopting it here makes it a brand face too.

Shimmer on the F

A highlight travelling around the outline of the F, with the rest of the word solid. This is why the face has to be settled first — the effect lives on the letterform’s contour, so it reads completely differently on a geometric F than on a serif one. Cormorant’s thin strokes give the highlight less to travel along; Inter’s give it more but less character. Same sweep on all four.

The tiles above are set large, which flatters everything. This is both of them at the sizes they would actually ship at — a 24px navbar mark, a 40px docs header, and a 96px hero. The top row is the one that decides: the gradient has almost no distance to travel at 24px, and Cormorant’s thin strokes are the other thing to look at there.
