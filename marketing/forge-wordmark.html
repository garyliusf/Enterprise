Twenty ways to put Forge next to bolt

All twenty are the same two elements — the bolt mark, and the word Forge to its right — so the only thing changing from tile to tile is the one decision named underneath it. Grouped into five families so they can be judged against their own kind rather than all at once. Use the controls above to change the ground colour and the size; every lockup scales from one variable, so nothing drifts out of proportion when you do.

Spacing & divider Same type treatment throughout — the only variable is what sits between the two words.

At working sizes

The tiles above are set large, which flatters everything. This is the same five candidates at the sizes they would actually ship at — a 24px navbar mark, a 40px docs header, and a 96px hero. Anything that stops reading in the top row is out, whatever it looks like at the bottom.
