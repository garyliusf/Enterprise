Forge, condensed

Three directions survived review: the Inter sans, the Cormorant italic, and Bebas Neue — uppercase-only, so Forge sets as caps against the mark’s lowercase. The sans and serif rounds lead the page, then those two through the six ramps, then twenty more faces, deliberately mixed across types.

Prototype page — not linked from anywhere and marked noindex. The bolt mark is images/bolt-logo.svg, inlined as a symbol and recoloured with currentColor so it follows the ground. “Forge” is live text (Inter for the gradient, Cormorant Garamond for the italic) rather than outlines, so either can be re-set without redrawing anything. The eighteen cut variations are in git history at b26a3e3 if any need to come back.
