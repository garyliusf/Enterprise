Forge, condensed

Bebas Neue and Staatliches were the two you kept, and they have something in common the rest of the set didn’t: both are uppercase-only, so Forge sets as caps against the mark’s lowercase. That contrast is the direction. Below, those two run through the six ramps, then twenty more condensed and caps faces, all set in caps. The earlier sans and serif rounds are still here too, at the foot of the page.
