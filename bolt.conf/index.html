Why Bolt

The AI that actually ships

Most AI coding tools generate code. Bolt generates apps — complete, tested, deployable ones.

01 —
What you'll build

Real full-stack apps with databases, auth, and production infrastructure — not toy examples.

  • Full-stack apps with one prompt
  • Database schemas and migrations
  • Auth, payments, and third-party APIs
  • Production-ready from day one
02 —
How fast you'll move

Skip the scaffolding, the boilerplate, the config files. Go from prompt to deployed in under five minutes.

  • First deploy in under 5 minutes
  • Iterate directly in the browser
  • No environment setup required
  • Git, CI/CD all pre-wired
03 —
Who it's for

Solo founder, design team, or enterprise engineering org — Bolt fits how you actually work.

  • Founders and indie hackers
  • Product designers who want to build
  • Engineering teams moving faster
  • Enterprises that need security + scale
